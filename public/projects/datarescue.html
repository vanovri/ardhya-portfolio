04 / Results

Results

  • Fewer manual validation errors: rule-based validation and normalization replace hand-checking of CSV/JSON rows.
  • Nothing silently lost: rejected rows are quarantined instead of dropped, so every repair can be reviewed and reversed.
  • Traceable changes: exported audit reports record what was changed and what was rejected.
  • Reproducible pipeline: the same rules run from the local workbench or the CLI, and behaviour is covered by automated tests (AI-assisted).
05 / Lessons

Lessons

  • Keeping rejected rows instead of dropping them makes repairs auditable.

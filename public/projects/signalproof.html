01 / Overview

What I built

An evidence-first low-voltage signal quality workbench that computes six deterministic metrics (DC offset, AC RMS, rail clipping, 48-52 Hz mains hum, timing jitter and capture duration) before validating bilingual explanations against fixed finding IDs.

Developed with AI coding assistance. Source code, reproduction instructions, a 3-minute narrated walkthrough on YouTube and tests are available via the links above.

02 / Evidence

What was checked

Verified with 60 passing Node.js tests, 15/15 synthetic evaluation scenarios, and 520 archived synthetic inference records (1,313,133 tokens across Baseten & Fireworks).

Verification date: 7 November 2026. Tests cover the included fixtures and recorded audit traces and do not establish production performance.

03 / Scope

What this demonstrates

Validates signal quality rules on synthetic and CSV bench captures (0.0-3.3 V default range). Passing deterministic checks and recorded LLM audit replays do not establish calibrated oscilloscope accuracy or hardware safety.

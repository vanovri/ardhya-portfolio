01 / Overview

What I built

An evidence-first low-voltage signal quality workbench that computes six deterministic metrics (DC offset, AC RMS, rail clipping, 48-52 Hz mains hum, timing jitter and slew/spikes) before validating bilingual explanations against fixed finding IDs.

Developed with AI coding assistance. Source code, reproduction instructions and tests are available in the linked repository.

03 / Scope

What this demonstrates

Validates signal quality rules on synthetic and CSV bench captures (0.0-3.3 V default range). Passing deterministic checks and recorded LLM audit replays do not establish calibrated oscilloscope accuracy or hardware safety.

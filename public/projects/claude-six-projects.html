01 / Overview

What I built

A browser suite inspired by a six-project showcase reference, with no frontend build step, unified under an overview hub and instant embedded iframe switcher:

  • 01 — FireTrace (3D Wildfire Spread Simulator): Procedural 3D San Gabriel Mountains terrain (`34.228°N, 118.144°W`) with hourly fire perimeter scrubber (`06:00`–`22:00`), animated wind particles, VLAT Phos-Chek retardant drops, spot-fire ignition and 3 synchronized telemetry charts (Fireline Intensity & Spread, Wind & Gusts, Fuel Moisture & RH).
  • 02 — Doodle Chess (Notebook Paper Chess): Lined notebook paper board with hand-drawn SVG pieces (Queen Bee, Star Blob Rook, Doodle Llama Knight, Acorn Bishop, Crowned King, Spotted Toadstool Pawn), red crayon selection rings, chess move generation and Minimax Alpha-Beta AI.
  • Notes-to-Font: an interactive preset-glyph demonstration with vector-path controls, text preview and SVG font-markup export. The interface illustrates a workflow. It does not process a real 120-page handwriting corpus.
  • 04 — Induction Bench (3D Magnetic Field Lab): Real-time 3D magnetic dipole field lines, draggable bar magnet (`N52`, `N42`, `SmCo28`, `C8`) and multi-turn solenoid (`Cu`, `Ag`, `Al`, `NiCr`), coupled with an analog zero-center galvanometer, dual-trace CRT oscilloscope ($\mathcal{E}(t)$ & $I(t)$) and live Flux Linkage $\Lambda(x)$ plot.
  • 05 — AngioView (3D Heart & Fluoroscopy Trainer): Synchronized dual-viewport trainer pairing a 3D anatomical heart and color-coded coronary arterial tree (`RCA`, `PDA`, `LMCA`, `LAD`, `D1/D2`, `S1`, `LCx`, `OM1`) with a real-time simulated C-arm X-ray fluoroscopy projection (`LAO/RAO` and `CRA/CAU` angles, 6 clinical presets, contrast dye bolus and LAD stenosis callout).
  • 06 — 20 Coded Poems (Animated Haiku Anthology): 20 nature haikus paired with charcoal-sketch procedural HTML5 Canvas animations, opening with Espalier Pear Blossom, Dew on a Silk Thread and Autumn Ink Feathering.

03 / Scope

What this demonstrates

Educational browser visualizations and interaction demos. The font studio uses predefined vector glyphs and exports SVG font markup. It does not OCR or train a font from 120 uploaded pages, and general desktop-font compatibility has not been established. Wildfire and medical scenes are illustrative models, not calibrated hazard forecasts, clinical training validation or diagnostic tools. JavaScript syntax checks do not establish cross-browser compatibility or scientific accuracy.

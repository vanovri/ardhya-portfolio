01 / Overview

What I built

A unified 7-in-1 static web application suite deployed on Vercel (radar-loker.vercel.app) and GitHub Pages, divided into four career-protection tools and three technical/legal laboratory tools:

  • 01 — Detektor Loker Red Flag (/index.html): Deterministic 18-rule scanner scoring Indonesian job postings from 0–100 across illegal diploma retention (SE Menaker No. M/5/HK.04.00/V/2025), recruitment fee scams, Telegram/WhatsApp task-deposit fraud, sub-UMP daily/monthly wage offers, and unverified recruiter identities, with instant text highlighting and PNG report card export.
  • 02 — Eksperimen 30 Hari AI Lamar Kerja (/eksperimen.html): Privacy-redacted public telemetry dashboard aggregating 70 real fresh-graduate engineering applications across 54 companies, funnel conversion rates, portal breakdowns (KarirJakarta, Glints, JobStreet, direct portals), and human-in-the-loop quality lessons.
  • 03 — Simulator Sidang Skripsi (/sidang.html): Extracts quantitative claims, sensors/controllers, and methodological gaps from an undergraduate thesis abstract, then runs a 3-examiner oral defense simulation (Prof. Bambang on methodology, Dr. Ratna on industry edge cases, Pak Hendra on statistical validity) with Web Speech API voice synthesis and real-time rubric scoring.
  • 04 — Peta Gaji Fresh Grad RI vs UMK (/gaji.html): Compares fresh-graduate salary offers against 2025/2026 city minimum wages (UMK) across 16 Indonesian industrial regions and 5 engineering/tech roles, estimates disposable savings after kos + living costs, and generates polite HR negotiation scripts.
  • 05 — Roast CV ala HRD (/roast-cv.html): Client-side CV bullet auditor checking quantitative impact density, active vs. passive Indonesian/English verbs, buzzword cliches, and PII leaks (16-digit NIK), paired with 3 reviewer personas (HRD Startup Jaksel, HRD Manufaktur, Mesin ATS) and an automatic Google-XYZ bullet rewriter.
  • 06 — Datasheet Register → C Driver Generator (/datasheet.html): Parses Markdown/TSV I2C & SPI register maps (including built-in INA219, MPU-6050, and BMP280 presets) into example .h bitmask headers, .c drivers for STM32 HAL (HAL_I2C_Mem_Read/Write) or ESP-IDF, and deterministic loopback unit tests.
  • 07 — Pasal & Kontrak Diff Explainer (/pasal.html): Longest-Common-Subsequence (LCS) word-level diff engine comparing UU Ketenagakerjaan vs. UU Cipta Kerja provisions and standard vs. predatory PKWT employment contracts, translating legal wording changes into concrete worker-rights impacts.

03 / Scope

What this demonstrates

Educational client-side rule engines and example code generation. Legal and salary values are a dated reference snapshot and require current primary-source verification before real decisions. Generated C drivers have not been validated on physical STM32 or ESP32 hardware. Rule-based CV and thesis feedback does not establish recruiter accuracy, fraud classification accuracy or examination outcomes.

03 / Real Hardware & Data Post-Mortems

4 Production Failure Modes Caught and Resolved on GTX 1650 Ti

  • transformers 5.x Silent 100% [UNK] Tokenizer Regression (Project 08): AutoTokenizer bound SentencePiece AlbertTokenizer onto indobert-lite-base-p1's WordPiece vocab.txt, collapsing accuracy to 37.60%. Switching to indobenchmark/indobert-base-p1 with an explicit BertTokenizer guard jumped held-out accuracy to 90.00% (+23.34% Macro-F1 over TF-IDF).
  • Turing TU117 cuDNN FP16 NaN & Inverted Focal Loss Alpha (Projects 02, 03, 09): FP16 mixed precision overflowed inside BatchNorm2d(1) Attention Gates and 14-band eval loops, while FocalDiceLoss(alpha=0.25) weighted background 3x above minority corrosion pixels (0.0000 Dice). Selective FP32 isolation, gradient clipping, and alpha=0.75 recovered Attention U-Net to 0.8739 Dice, Corrosion U-Net to 0.7720 Dice at 197.0 FPS, and Landslide4Sense to 0.9771 Pixel ROC-AUC.
  • WESAD 6.6 GB Nested-Array Host RAM Blowup (Project 07): Loading the full repo expanded 6.58 GB dense-overlap windows into 8+ GB RAM. Targeting the 66.9 MB wesad_4_3_120.parquet shard with unified 16 Hz zero-phase SOS Butterworth DSP cut peak VRAM to 85.7 MB with zero subject leakage.
  • Landslide4Sense HDF5 Packager Skip & HTTP 429 Limit (Project 09): Generic HDF5 dataset builders skipped paired .h5 files while 1,000 individual file requests hit HF 429 rate limits. Reading embedded raw HDF5 bytes directly from 6 official auto-converted Parquet shards via h5py.File(io.BytesIO(...)) resolved both bottlenecks cleanly.

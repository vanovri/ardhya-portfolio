01 / Privacy & Data Protection

Zero-Tracking & Cookie Policy

No advertising cookies or behavioral tracking. This website (ardhyanovri.my.id) is a static engineering portfolio. It does not load third-party advertising pixels, cross-site tracking cookies, session replay scripts or behavioral analytics.

Client-side execution only. Interactive demonstrations—such as the EMG Signal & Serial Lab and local search—run entirely in your browser memory using client-side JavaScript. No inputs entered into the interactive labs are transmitted to an external server.

Voluntary contact & public CV redaction. Reaching out via Contact opens your local email client (mailto:ardhyasan@gmail.com); no contact form messages are stored in an intermediary web database. To protect personal privacy on the public web, the downloadable public CV (cv-ardhya.pdf) intentionally omits personal phone numbers and street addresses, which are shared directly during formal recruitment processes.

02 / Engineering & Claim Scope Disclaimer

Evidence-First Claim Boundaries

Every technical project, internship record and research artifact on this site separates configured parameters from measured results, and individual contributions from team or supervisor scope:

Undergraduate Thesis (EMG)
My individual scope on the rehabilitation-hand thesis (Diponegoro University, 2026) covers the SEN0240 + STM32F411CEU6 EMG acquisition firmware (12-bit ADC with DMA configured at a nominal 4 kHz), cascaded digital filtering (20 Hz HPF, 500 Hz LPF, 50 Hz notch), RMS/MAV feature extraction (400-sample / ~100 ms window) and the complete Python/Tkinter monitoring GUI. The FES stimulator hardware (Aldin) and fuzzy/FSM control design (Zain) are distinct team contributions. The GUI self-test (10/10 check groups) verifies software parsing, safety state logic and CSV logging; it does not claim clinical validation or medical-device certification.
Airport Electrical Internship
During my Jan–Feb 2025 internship at PT Angkasa Pura Indonesia (InJourney Airports — Soekarno-Hatta International Airport Electrical Utility), my activities consisted of supervised preventive maintenance observation, Genset/panel parameter checklist logging and technical orientation across UPS, CCRC, HVD and LVD systems. I do not claim independent high-voltage switching authority or protection-relay commissioning.
ENVIID Web Development
As sole web developer for PT ENVIID Karbon Solusi (enviid.web.id), I built the bilingual corporate site, interactive biochar carbon removal calculator and digital MRV workflow walkthrough. The calculator and dMRV pages are interactive web scenario models and traceability demonstrations for a project in development—not a certified carbon credit issuance registry.
Proposals & Simulations
Academic and competition proposals (Nusantara SolarWing, Timor-Leste Rural Technology, GovTech/Gov-AI, IoT Biofiltration, PLTA Jatigede) are documented strictly as conceptual or model-based studies without field-deployment claims. Open-source benchmark suites and browser simulations state their exact synthetic test fixtures and verification commands.
AI Assistance Disclosure
Coding agents (Claude, OpenAI Codex, Google Antigravity) assist with scaffolding, refactoring, UI iteration and documentation. I review, test and take responsibility for the published code and factual accuracy.
03 / Security & Third-Party Services

Security Headers & Infrastructure

Hosting & Transport Security. Static assets and fonts are self-hosted and served over HTTPS via Vercel edge infrastructure with HTTP Strict Transport Security (Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=63072000; includeSubDomains).

Browser Hardening. Responses enforce a strict Content-Security-Policy (default-src 'self', frame-ancestors 'none', object-src 'none'), X-Frame-Options: DENY, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin, Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin and a restrictive Permissions-Policy disabling camera, microphone, geolocation, USB, WebSerial, WebBluetooth and Topics/FLoC APIs.

External Links & Security Contact. Outbound links to GitHub, LinkedIn and enviid.web.id open with rel="noopener" and are governed by their respective platforms' privacy policies. Security researchers may report issues via /.well-known/security.txt or ardhyasan@gmail.com.

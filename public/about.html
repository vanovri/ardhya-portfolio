About / Profile

About Ardhya.

I'm Ardhya Novri Ramadhan, based in Bekasi. I hold a Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical Engineering from Diponegoro University (graduated 29 September 2026, GPA 3.43/4.00). I started with electronics, wiring circuits, assisting in the basic electronics lab and teaching PCB work at a student workshop, and software grew around that hardware.

I served as a Basic Electronics Practicum Assistant for two consecutive semesters in the 2024/2025 academic year, the odd semester in 2024 and the even semester in 2025. I guided students through laboratory activities and electronic component use.

My thesis made that link concrete. On a team building a rehabilitation-hand system, I owned the EMG subsystem: STM32F411 firmware in C that samples a SEN0240 sensor at a nominal 4 kHz through timer-triggered DMA, filters the signal (20 Hz HPF, 500 Hz LPF, 50 Hz notch) and computes RMS/MAV features, plus the full Python/Tkinter interface that plots, monitors and logs it. Teammates handled the stimulator and fuzzy control. I keep configured values and measured results as separate claims.

Before that, I spent eight weeks at Soekarno-Hatta International Airport with PT Angkasa Pura Indonesia, learning UPS/converter systems and electrical protection, reading single-line diagrams (SLDs) and using basic AutoCAD. At Redesma Technologies I built, on my own, IoT temperature-monitoring (ESP32, DS18B20) and DC motor-control prototypes and a UART communication project, and presented them at my KP (internship) seminar. During community service in Mutihan, Klaten, I helped install a solar-powered Smart Owl House.

Since October 2026 I've been CTO / Technical Lead at PT ENVIID Karbon Solusi (ENVIID Carbon Solutions), where I am the sole developer of the company website, built with AI assistance, enviid.web.id. I also maintain open-source tools, including uart-fixture-kit, DataRescue, RepairGate and ApplyProof. I use coding agents openly, I say where they helped, and I verify their output with tests.

Recent releases extend that work into cross-language numerical checks, WebGPU simulation, parametric CAD and browser interfaces. Each project links to its source, verification and stated limitations. Browse the project collection.
